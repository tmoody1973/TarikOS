233 commits in eight days. Here are all 28 things my personal AI assistant can do.

Her name is Zola. I reach her by voice on a page, by text, or by calling a phone number.

THE SPINE

1. Telos: a structured record of what I'm actually working toward. Mission, goals with real finish lines, problems, strategies. She interviews me for it once, then keeps it current from how I actually spend my days.
2. A morning brief assembled before I wake up. It opens with my goals, not my inbox.
3. The brief writes its own opening paragraph. Something reads all twelve sections and tells me what changed, what needs me, what's drifting.
4. A Sunday review she walks me through, asking whether each goal still stands.

THE WORKSPACE

5. Read full email threads in the app, across accounts.
6. She drafts replies in my voice. There is no send button she can reach.
7. Her own email address. Strangers who write to it reach an agent, not me.
8. A real writing workspace. Five document types, version history, DOCX export.
9. She suggests edits by quoting my own words back. She never changes my writing.
10. A project board backed by a real tracker, so I never open the tracker.
11. Say "start a project with these five tasks" and she reads it back before creating anything.
12. 4,370 contacts, synced nightly. Two matches is a question, never a guess.
13. Turn any brief into a file and share it with an expiry, a download cap, and a kill switch.
14. Habits with no streak counter anywhere. The number is how often I came back after a gap.
15. Calendar by voice, with a read-back before every write.
16. Reminders that reach me by text or email.

HOW I REACH HER

17. Voice in the browser.
18. A wake word I trained myself for under a dollar. 161 KB, runs locally.
19. She hangs up when the conversation ends, not when I press something.
20. A real phone number. She answers with the same brain and the same tools.
21. Text, with deliberately fewer tools than voice.
22. A browser she drives while I watch, and I can take the wheel.
23. A console showing the waveform, the transcript, and which tools are firing.

RUNNING WHILE I SLEEP

24. Conversations consolidated nightly, recalled by meaning rather than keywords.
25. Voice journaling, mined overnight for signals about my goals.
26. Multi-step research on a schedule, delivered as cards.

HOW I KNOW IT WORKS

27. Every tool has a health light and an off switch that blocks it at the door.
28. 980 tests, and an eval harness that replays things I actually said to score which tool she reaches for. Two identical runs disagree about 9 percent of the time, so it reports that noise rather than letting me read meaning into a small move.

The rule underneath all of it: she can do a great deal on her own, and almost nothing on her own authority. She drafts, proposes and prepares. I release.
